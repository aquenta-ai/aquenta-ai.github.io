Verbasignal

Every word on the radio, turned into an event you can trust.

Signals from the verbose.

Ground operations run on voice. Pushbacks, start-ups, taxi clearances and line-ups are spoken, heard and then keyed in by hand, late or not at all. Verbasignal sits beside your existing radio and turret systems, listens to every channel, and emits a clean, canonical event stream your operational systems can consume directly.

  • Canonical events, not transcripts. Typed movement milestones with callsign, stand, position, timing, confidence and full provenance, published against a versioned schema.
  • Episodic and semantic memory. Built around a memory of what your site has heard and confirmed (every operator correction becomes an episode) and what it knows (airline aliases, stands, taxiways). It gets sharper with every shift.
  • AI interprets, rules authorise. A deterministic state machine is the final authority. A model can never write operational state on its own.
  • Emit early, revise honestly. Events go out fast as provisional, then settle. Unclear calls are delivered as unresolved, never silently dropped or guessed.
  • Built for real radio. Push-to-talk gating, cross-position de-duplication and defences against silence hallucinations, before a single word is transcribed.

Skyweave

One sky. One picture. Ask it anything.

Traffic, weather, NOTAMs and infrastructure, woven into a single operating picture.

Air navigation and airport teams juggle separate systems for surveillance, weather, notices and capacity, often split across several flight regions. Skyweave fuses them into one read-only picture for every role, and puts an AI copilot on top that you can simply talk to.

  • Talk to the airspace. A voice and text copilot that answers from live data, flies the map to what matters and drafts situation briefings in seconds.
  • Grounded, never invented. The copilot can only speak about flights, stations and hazards that exist in the data. Every answer cites its source.
  • Correlation and prediction. Aircraft inside hazard cells, flight-category changes at the field, and delay risk per airport with a stated reason and a suggested mitigation.
  • Read-only by design. Skyweave sits above your air traffic systems with no write path into them. It informs; it never issues a clearance.
  • Replay anything. Live, simulated and recorded feeds share one schema. Scrub the timeline to see the picture as it stood at any moment.

Interpres

Every voice heard. Every word on the record.

Live transcription and translation, built for courtrooms and hearings.

When a witness speaks a different language from the court, justice slows down and the record gets harder to check. Interpres streams faithful, court-appropriate translation onto the bench screen as people speak, and keeps a source transcript beside every translated line so nothing has to be taken on trust.

  • Live, in about three seconds. Provisional text appears as words are spoken, then settles into final text at each pause.
  • Transcribe first, then translate. The source-language transcript is kept and checkable on its own, so every translated line traces back to what was actually said.
  • Legal register, protected details. A refinement pass applies legal terminology and guards the things courts care about most: names, numbers, dates and negation.
  • Case memory, strictly walled. Later hearings of the same case remember its people, places and terms. Context never overrides the spoken word, and nothing crosses between cases.
  • Sovereign and tamper-evident. Fully on-premise with no internet dependency, active-active resilience and a signed audit envelope for every session.
